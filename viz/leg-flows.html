Leg flows

"One leg, many journeys" looked at a single flight. This looks at every route into and out of a hub at once, for the whole of February 2027. Each row is one airport's flights with the hub, and the bar is everyone on them, split by where they were really going. Grey is people going no further than the hub. Each colour is a region beyond it, and each small block inside a colour is one city.

Patterns in this view

    Limits. Every pattern here was put there by the simulator: demand between two cities follows the size of each and the distance between them, about a third of traffic connects, and a connection is only sold if the route is at most 1.5 times the direct distance, which is why an airport's colours are nearly all from the far side of the hub. Passengers are counted once per direction of travel. Routes with no flights to the hub do not appear.

    Data: odysee-data-warehouse/src/utilities/synthetic_airline run at full scale for 1–28 February 2027, aggregated by onboarding/tools/viz_data.py --journeys. No client data.
