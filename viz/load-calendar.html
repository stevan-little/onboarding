Load calendar

A year of the synthetic airline, one square per day. Weeks run across and weekdays run down, so a pale or dark row is a weekday habit and a patch of colour is a season or a holiday. The heavy outlines are the months. Each calendar is a different slice of the same airline on one shared colour scale: red is low, yellow is middling, green is high.

What the year looks like

    Limits. The simulator only has a schedule for February 2027, so for every other month it flies that same February schedule and varies only the demand. The seasons, the weekday rhythm, the Thanksgiving and Christmas lifts and Florida's opposite season are all fixed multipliers in its code, made visible here; a real year would also have schedule changes, weather and events. Red-to-green was asked for and reads quickly, but it is the hardest scale for colour-blind readers; hover any day for its number. Revenue per seat is fare money booked to the flights, with connecting fares split by miles, divided by every seat flown.

    Data: odysee-data-warehouse/src/utilities/synthetic_airline run at full scale for every day of 2027 by onboarding/tools/viz_data.py --year. No client data.
